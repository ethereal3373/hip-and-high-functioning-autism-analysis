# Personal notes taken from the paper

## What is the study about?
The goal of this study was to characterize a pediatric sample of HIP (High Cognitive Potential) individuals in direct comparison to HFA (High Functioning Autism) individuals and a neurotypical developmental (NTD) group.
This study was ran not only from a clinical perspective, but also a neurophysiological point of view, to be able to better descrive the HIP phenotype compared to HFA and NTD children.
The main reasoning for this study was the lack of prior empirical studies on it and the growing interest in the topic in recent times.

## The study itself
The study was ran in Roma, Italy, between 2019 and 2020, with a total of 43 children ranging from 6 to 16 years old.
The only conditions required were respectively a HIP diagnosis, a diagnosis of autism spectrum disorder without language and/or cognitive imparirment (IQ above 70).
The children were then tested on different levels, for the result data to then be analysed by a defined group of italian psychiatrists.
